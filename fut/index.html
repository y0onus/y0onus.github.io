About Futboalha

The game. The news. The Maldives.

Futboalha is the definitive football platform built for the Maldivian football community — and every fan around the world who lives and breathes the beautiful game.

Track your club, follow live matches, read the latest breaking news, and stay on top of every tournament — all from one beautifully crafted app.

From the Dhivehi League to the Champions League, we bring you every kick of the ball with data that moves as fast as the game itself.
